Privacy Policy

Local-First Architecture

Chroma is a 100% client-side application. All color calculations and conversions happen entirely in your browser. No data is ever transmitted to, stored on, or processed by any server.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The color values you input and the palettes you generate remain on your device.

Cookies and Tracking

Chroma does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any form of user tracking. We do not use third-party analytics services.

Browser Storage

The application may use browser localStorage to save your preferences (such as the last used color). This data is stored locally on your device and is never shared with any external service.

Third-Party Services

Chroma is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which may use standard server logs for security and performance monitoring. These logs do not contain personal information or color data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us through Calyvent at calyvent.com
